# Sources — how each story on the page was verified

Edition: **Thursday 19 November 2026** (make.fun news-image-20261119).
Date rule used for every item: the story **happened, or was first
reported, on Thursday 19 November 2026**. Anything that belonged to an
earlier day was dropped — most notably Voyager 1's one-light-day
crossing, which happened on Wednesday 18 November (NASA via

## Lead

1. **Shenzhen — the first three-way summit.** USA Today (19 Nov,
   updated within the hour): Trump, Xi and Putin held the first-ever
   trilateral talks on the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen, about an
   hour, spanning Ukraine, Iran, nuclear weapons, trade and AI; Trump
   met Putin (~40 minutes) and Xi separately first; no immediate
   breakthrough expected. Also CBS News / BBC via One Turn, Aju Press
   (meeting times: Xi–Trump 12:45 Shenzhen, three-way 15:30 at Kylin
   Villa). Cover illustration is our own, first drawn for the
   18 November edition, where the meeting was previewed.
