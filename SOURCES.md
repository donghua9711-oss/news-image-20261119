# Sources — how each story on the page was verified

Edition: **Thursday 19 November 2026** (make.fun news-image-20261119).
Date rule used for every item: the story **happened, or was first
reported, on Thursday 19 November 2026**. Anything that belonged to an
earlier day was dropped — most notably Voyager 1's one-light-day
crossing, which happened on Wednesday 18 November (NASA via
Smithsonian / USA Today / NPR, 17–18 Nov) and already appeared in our
18 November edition, and Cyclone Akvan's landfall preparations, also
an 18 November story.

The page was rebuilt on 20 November on the structure of the
edition's winning pages: a navy masthead, a rainbow region band,
a UTC timeline, three lead cards, two rows of four cards, a
pinned world map and the remaining stories as one-line briefs.
The stories are the same 34, renumbered in reading order;
stories 1–11 are drawn as cards and 12–34 run as briefs. The
numbers below are the numbers on the rebuilt page.

   updated within the hour): Trump, Xi and Putin held the first-ever
   trilateral talks on the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen, about an
   hour, spanning Ukraine, Iran, nuclear weapons, trade and AI; Trump
   met Putin (~40 minutes) and Xi separately first; no immediate
   breakthrough expected. Also CBS News / BBC via One Turn, Aju Press

   UTC timeline uses the Shenzhen times converted to UTC (04:45 and
   07:30), the 2.3m Twitch figure's 12:15 Paris time (11:15 UTC),
   and Nasdaq's 14:30 UTC open for ANT's first trades.
